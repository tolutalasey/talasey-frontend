# Using the content editor

Open `/content` on the site and sign in. On the left is every kind of content: the homepage, brand, project and range pages, guides, questions and answers, inspiration photos, navigation, site settings, redirects, email wording, and approved legal and finance wording.

## Drafts and publishing

- **Save draft** keeps your work. The site keeps showing the published version until it is published again.
- Contributors save drafts; an editor publishes them. Only the owner publishes legal and finance wording.
- Before publishing, anything missing or too long is listed at the top, such as a search title that isn't 30 to 60 characters. Fix the list and publish.
- **Preview the draft** shows guides and pages as the site will, before anyone else sees them.
- **Take off the site** unpublishes but keeps the draft. **History** shows every change and can put an earlier version back.

## Filling in fields

- Paragraphs: leave a blank line between them.
- Lists such as steps or key points: one per line.
- Rows such as questions and answers: there is always one empty row at the bottom for a new one. To remove a row, empty its fields and save.
- Photos: choose a file and describe what it shows for people who can't see it.
- Web addresses: lower-case words joined by hyphens, like `how-to-lay-a-porcelain-patio`. No dates or codes.

## Built-in copy

Until something is published here, the site shows its own placeholder copy for that page, marked so search engines ignore it. Publishing an entry with the same web address replaces it.
